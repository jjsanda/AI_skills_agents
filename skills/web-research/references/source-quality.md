# Source quality rubric

Use this to score each source before relying on it. Loaded on demand (kept out
of `SKILL.md`).

## Dimensions

| Dimension | High | Low |
|---|---|---|
| **Authority** | Primary source, recognized expert, peer-reviewed, official data | Anonymous, unknown site, content farm |
| **Evidence** | Cites data/methods you can check | Assertion with no support |
| **Recency** | Current for the topic's pace of change | Stale where the field moved on |
| **Independence** | Reports original reporting/analysis | Re-publishes another source verbatim |
| **Bias / incentive** | Discloses conflicts; even-handed | Strong undisclosed stake in the claim |

## Quick scoring

- **High**: strong on Authority + Evidence, reasonably current and independent.
- **Medium**: credible but secondary, or strong but dated.
- **Low**: weak authority or no checkable evidence — corroborate before using.

## Triangulation rule

Treat a claim as established only when **two or more independent, credible**
sources agree. Two outlets reprinting the same press release count as **one**
source. When credible sources disagree, report the disagreement rather than
picking a side silently.
